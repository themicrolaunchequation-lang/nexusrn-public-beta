What the demo helps you practice

The free demo introduces the type of reasoning tested by the Next Generation NCLEX: recognizing cues, analyzing clinical data, prioritizing hypotheses, choosing safe actions, and evaluating outcomes.

NGN item formats included

NexusRN is designed around NGN-style formats such as matrix questions, bow-tie reasoning, select-all-that-apply, drop-down logic, highlight-style cue recognition, trend interpretation, calculation, and unfolding case practice.

EHR-style clinical context

Instead of isolated facts, learners review patient information such as notes, vital signs, labs, orders, and scenario changes so decisions are made in clinical context.
