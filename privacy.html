Privacy Notice

This public-preview notice explains what NexusRN does and does not promise before production accounts, payments, analytics, and institutional administration are activated.

Do not enter real patient information

NexusRN is for exam-preparation practice. Do not enter protected health information, patient-identifying details, real clinical records, or confidential school/institution data into any note, prompt, or practice field.

What may be stored locally

  • Interface preferences, such as theme or local settings.
  • Local browser study state used by the preview experience.
  • Demo/practice state on the same browser only.

What is not active yet

  • No production account system.
  • No cross-device progress sync.
  • No payment checkout in this public-preview package.
  • No institutional roster or school admin portal.

Before paid launch

A production privacy policy must be finalized for authentication, payments, analytics, support, cookies, institutional accounts, data retention, and deletion/export requests.
