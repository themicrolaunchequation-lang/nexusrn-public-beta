Educational Disclaimer

NexusRN is built for nursing exam-preparation practice and clinical-judgment learning. It is not a medical device, clinical decision-support system, diagnostic tool, or licensure authority.

Do not use for real patient care

Scenarios, vitals, labs, medications, and rationales are for simulated education. Do not apply app outputs to real patients or clinical emergencies.

No exam result guarantee

Practice performance and readiness signals may help learners reflect, but they do not guarantee NCLEX eligibility, exam readiness, passing, or licensing outcomes.

Content review expectation

Before paid or institutional launch, every question and case should have review metadata, reviewer history, source/guideline references where appropriate, and scheduled re-review dates.
