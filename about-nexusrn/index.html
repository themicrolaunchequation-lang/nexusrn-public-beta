About NexusRN

NexusRN is an independent educational study project focused on helping nursing learners prepare for the Next Generation NCLEX through structured practice, clinical judgment reasoning, and clear study pathways.

What NexusRN is

NexusRN is built to support NCLEX NGN preparation with public study guides, practice-oriented explanations, and protected interactive learning experiences. The public pages are designed to help learners understand NGN concepts before they move into deeper practice.

What NexusRN is not

NexusRN is not the official NCLEX, NCSBN, or a board of nursing website. It does not replace official exam guidance, nursing school instruction, clinical supervision, or professional judgment. Learners should always verify exam rules and official policies with NCLEX and NCSBN.

Why the domain says Health Quality Leader

Health Quality Leader is the public domain currently hosting NexusRN. To reduce confusion, the site uses the identity “NexusRN by Health Quality Leader” on trust and study pages.
