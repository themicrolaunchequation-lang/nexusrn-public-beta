Preview Limitations

This page sets clear expectations so early users understand what NexusRN can and cannot do in the current public-preview package.

Available now

  • Updated landing page and public demo routing.
  • Restricted Public Demo with representative NGN samplers.
  • Decision Lab preview routes for Method, Priority Models, Readiness, Study Planner, and Stress Reset.
  • Join Beta and trust/legal pages.

Not active yet

  • No production accounts.
  • No cross-device progress sync.
  • Automated backend entitlement is pending; manual beta access confirmation is used.
  • No institutional admin portal.

How to interpret scores

Readiness, diagnostics, and feedback are educational signals for practice and reflection. They are not predictions, guarantees, licensure decisions, or clinical advice.
